# Grocery Management System

🚀 A complete web-based Grocery Management System built using Python and Django for managing products, inventory, batches, customers, orders, and grocery store operations efficiently.

---

# 📌 Project Overview

The Grocery Management System is designed to simplify grocery store management through a centralized platform that handles:

* Product and inventory management
* Batch and stock tracking
* Customer management
* Order and billing management
* Product variants and pricing
* Reports and analytics

This system helps grocery stores reduce manual work, improve stock handling, and manage day-to-day operations efficiently.

---

# ✅ Main Features

## 🛒 Product Management

* Add, edit, and delete products
* Product image management
* Product variant support
* Category management
* Unit type management

## 📦 Inventory & Batch Management

* Batch-wise stock tracking
* Expiry date management
* Quantity management
* Low stock handling
* Inventory updates

## 👥 Customer Management

* Add and manage customers
* Customer profile management
* Customer order tracking

## 🧾 Order & Billing System

* Order creation and management
* Billing and invoice handling
* Price and quantity calculations

## 📊 Admin Dashboard

* Dashboard overview
* Product statistics
* Inventory monitoring
* Sales management
